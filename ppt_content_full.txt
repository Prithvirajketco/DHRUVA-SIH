What is the problem?
1. Difficulty in identifying high-risk landslide/flood zones in advance
2. Lack of localized, real-time risk information
3. Delayed or non-actionable early warnings
4. Difficulty prioritizing the most critical locations for response
5. Roads and evacuation routes becoming unsafe or blocked
6. Fragmented disaster information from multiple sources
7. Communication/network failure during disasters
8. Rapidly changing ground and environmental conditions
